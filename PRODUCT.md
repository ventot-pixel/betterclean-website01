# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

BetterClean serves households in Tampere and nearby areas who want careful, reliable home cleaning without vague pricing or rushed results. Visitors are typically homeowners, apartment residents, busy professionals, families, and quality-focused customers comparing local cleaning options.

## Product Purpose

The website converts local cleaning interest into quote requests and booking requests for BetterClean. Success means visitors quickly understand what BetterClean offers, trust the service enough to make contact, and send enough detail for a personal quote or booking confirmation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, careful, trustworthy. BetterClean should feel local, polished, practical, and more professional than generic cleaning listings.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site feel like a discount marketplace, a rushed gig-service app, a generic stock-photo cleaning template, or a luxury brand detached from real household work. Avoid cheap-price language, exaggerated claims, slangy Finnish, and visual clutter.

## Design Principles

- Lead with clarity: the visitor should understand the service, price frame, and next step quickly.
- Make trust visible: local area, clear limits, careful process, and personal follow-up matter more than hype.
- Protect premium positioning: limited promotions can exist, but the main brand should not look cheap.
- Keep action simple: every page should make it easy to request a quote or ask a question.
- Match the existing BetterClean system: green brand palette, Sora headings, Inter body copy, bilingual Finnish-first content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, readable contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion-friendly interactions, and bilingual Finnish and English content.
