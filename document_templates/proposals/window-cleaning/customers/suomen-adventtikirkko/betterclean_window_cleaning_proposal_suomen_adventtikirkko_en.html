1. Work Description

BetterClean offers the client professional window cleaning services at Satakunnankatu 23, Tampere. The work will be carried out with professional tools, according to the agreed schedule, and to a clearly defined quality level.

This proposal includes two options. The client may choose the option that best fits the site's needs.

2. Scope of Work

Option A - Full Service (60 hours)

  • washing window glass surfaces inside and outside
  • drying and finishing the glass to a streak-free result
  • wiping window frames and casings
  • wiping window sills
  • delivery of cleaning agents and work equipment to the site
  • documentation of working time, including start time, finish time and completed areas
  • final review with the contact person

Option B - Exterior Glass Only (40 hours)

  • washing only the exterior window glass surfaces, covering 2 glass surfaces
  • drying and finishing exterior glass surfaces to a streak-free result
  • wiping exterior window frames and casings
  • wiping window sills
  • delivery of cleaning agents and work equipment to the site
  • documentation of working time
  • final review with the contact person

Not included in Option B: washing interior window glass surfaces or washing Venetian blinds.
